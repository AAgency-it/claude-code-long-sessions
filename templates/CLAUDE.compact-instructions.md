## Compact instructions

<!-- Paste this section into your CLAUDE.md (project or ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md). Claude Code applies it to

When the conversation is compacted, write the summary like this:

- The first line is `STATE FILE: <full path of this session's state file>`, if the session has one.
- Keep the decisions of the person, with the date and their exact words.
- Keep every approval given and every approval still awaited, and what each one covers.
- Keep commits, branches, file paths, numbers and deadlines, exactly as they were written.
- Keep the safety constraints in full: what must never be done, what needs a go-ahead first.
- Do not paste the content of long files, tool output or logs. The path is enough.
- The last line is the next step.
